Air Fryer Dijon Salmon

The "Dijon-Glazed Tech Stack": High-speed flavor deployment with a mustard-middleware crust.

395Calories
34gProtein
3gCarbs
25gFat
Health Benefits: Mustard seeds contain glucosinolates, which provide a "firewall" against cellular inflammation.

Ingredients

  • 2 Salmon Fillets (6oz each)
  • 1.5 tbsp Dijon Mustard
  • 1 tsp Honey or Maple Syrup
  • 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Smoked Paprika
  • Fresh Parsley, chopped
  • Salt & Black Pepper to taste

Instructions

1 Initialize Environment

Preheat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Ensuring the hardware is warm before execution prevents sticking.

2 The Middleware Mix

Whisk together Dijon, honey, garlic powder, and paprika in a small bowl until the consistency is smooth.

3 The API Call

Pat salmon dry and season with salt/pepper. Brush a thick layer of the glaze over the top of each fillet.

4 Execution & Final Render

Air fry for 8–10 minutes. Garnish with parsley and let rest for 2 minutes before serving.
